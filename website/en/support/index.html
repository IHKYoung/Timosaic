A little help with Timosaic

Record, edit and save

Choose an activity in Today and paint across the time blocks. Each stroke saves automatically when you lift your finger. Undo or erase a mistake, or hold a block to adjust its times. Quick Capture (+) keeps a draft until you tap the button to save your moment.

A diary for the whole day

Open Diary in Today to keep your thoughts, mood, little joys and plans for tomorrow. Notes attached to a stretch of time are kept separately from your daily diary. Find both by date in Review.

Exports and changing devices

In Me, open data export to save a complete JSON file or a CSV of your time entries. Version 1.0 has no cloud sync or import. Uninstalling removes local app data. Save exports before uninstalling or changing devices; they are copies for safekeeping, not files you can currently restore into the app.

What do the insights mean?

Statistics come only from entries you choose to record. You can adjust your sleep reference and work reminders; these are not medical advice. Blank time is not treated as rest, and comparisons are withheld when there is not enough recorded time.

Still need a hand?

Tell us your iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue and any error message. Please do not upload diary entries, complete exports or other personal information.
