A LITTLE HELP

A few things you might wonder

Does the app need to stay open?＋

No. Timosaic is a manual time journal, not an activity monitor. Paint a moment whenever you remember, or revisit a previous day.

Do I have to fill every block?＋

Not at all. Recording is a way to understand yourself, not a test. A little time spent resting belongs in the picture, too.

What if I paint the wrong block?＋

Undo, redo or erase in Today. Hold a block to edit its times. Adjacent entries of the same activity form one stretch on your timeline.

Do my entries sync across devices?＋

Version 1.0 stores entries locally and does not include cloud sync or import. Export full JSON or time entries as CSV. Export important data before uninstalling.
